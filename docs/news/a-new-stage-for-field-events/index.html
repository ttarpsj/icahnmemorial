A new stage for field events

The infield is designed so every take-off, throw and landing can be felt from the stands.

Every good meeting begins before the starter raises a hand. It begins with the practical work of making people feel expected: a clear programme, a welcoming gate, a place where the action is close.

This original demonstration story introduces the visual and editorial approach behind Icahn Memorial. It is not a report of a real competition or an announcement of athlete participation.

As the venue, team and partners take shape, this space can become the living journal of a real event.
